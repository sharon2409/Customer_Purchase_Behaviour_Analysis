# Customer Purchase Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, product performance, and customer value. Python-based data analysis techniques were used to clean, explore, visualize, and segment the customer data.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective is to analyze customer purchase patterns and build a customer segmentation model based on purchasing behavior.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce transaction records with the following information:

* Transaction ID
* User Name
* Age
* Country
* Product Category
* Purchase Amount
* Payment Method
* Transaction Date

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The following data preparation steps were performed:

* Loaded the dataset using Pandas
* Checked the dataset structure
* Checked for missing values
* Checked for duplicate records
* Converted `Transaction_Date` into a proper datetime format
* Created a monthly time-period column for sales analysis

## 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project analyzed:

* Total revenue
* Average purchase amount
* Total transactions
* Total customers
* Top 10 customers
* Product category performance
* Monthly sales trends
* Payment method usage
* Customer purchasing patterns

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into three segments based on their purchasing behavior:

* **High Value** — customers with comparatively high total spending
* **Regular** — customers with multiple purchases who are not classified as high-value
* **One-time Buyers** — customers with only one transaction

## 💰 Top 10% Customer Analysis

The top 10% of customers were identified based on total spending.

Their contribution to overall revenue was calculated to understand how strongly high-value customers influence total business revenue.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Customers contributing the highest revenue
* Product categories generating higher sales
* Periods with higher and lower sales
* Distribution of customer segments
* Revenue contribution from the highest-value customers
* Customer purchasing and payment patterns

> The numerical findings and percentages are based on the results generated from the uploaded dataset.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

* Focus retention strategies on high-value customers
* Encourage one-time buyers to make repeat purchases
* Monitor high-performing product categories
* Plan marketing campaigns around stronger sales periods
* Use customer segmentation for targeted promotions
* Track customer spending patterns to improve customer value
